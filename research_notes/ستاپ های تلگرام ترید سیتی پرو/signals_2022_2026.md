# Trade City Pro (@tradecity_pro): trade calls and setups, 2022–2026

Files in this folder:
- `extract_signals_2022_2026.py` is the extractor. Its rules are followed by a `MANUAL` dict of hand corrections. Re-run it with `python3 extract_signals_2022_2026.py`.
- `signals_2022_2026.csv` holds the rows: 728 setup rows and 291 chart-only rows.
- `signals_2022_2026_stats.json` holds every number quoted below.

Post URL pattern: `https://t.me/tradecity_pro/<id>`. Persian is quoted as written in the posts. Glosses are mine.

## 1. Scope and method

| Item | Count |
|---|---|
| Archive posts dated 2022-01-01 to 2026-10-17 (`main_all.jsonl`) | 18,599 |
| Reposts or truncated copies folded into the original (text prefix match; copies ending in "…") | 1,831 |
| "pinned" notices skipped | 328 |
| Posts in the pre-filtered `main_signals_B_2022_2026.jsonl` | 597 |
| **Posts with at least one extracted setup** | **460** |
| Setup posts that the B filter **missed**, recovered from `main_all` | 112 (24 %) |
| **Setup rows**: one per post × side × trigger level | **728** (681 have a numeric trigger) |
| Rows that are not a restatement of the same symbol/side/level within 14 days | 659 |
| Chart-only posts: a symbol plus chart, image or link, with no setup text | 291 |
| Post-hoc "result" posts (نتیجه / Update), kept out of the setup rows | 10 |

How the rows were built:
- **Normalisation.** Persian and Arabic digits are mapped to ASCII. `٫` and `0/2313`-style separators become decimal points. A Persian comma used as a decimal (`2,149` for ATOM = 2.149) is decided per post. `هزار` (thousand) suffixes are expanded. ZWNJ and Arabic ی/ک are unified.
- **Clause parsing.** Each post is split into clauses. Every number gets a role: trigger level, descriptive level, target (`به سمت/تا/تارگت`), stop (`استاپ/حد ضرر/Sl`), validity level (`تا زمانی که بالای/زیر X`), box edge (`بین X و Y`), RSI level, or "already activated" (`فعال شد`). Side is taken from the side word (لانگ/شورت/long/short/استاپ بای/پوزیشن سل…). When needed it comes from direction cues (زیر/حمایت → short, بالای/مقاومت → long).
- **Exclusions.** Negated clauses are dropped, for example «تریگر … نیست», «پوزیشن شورت باز نمیکنم» and «این به این معنا نیست که…». So are management-only clauses (سیو سود, ریسک فری).
- **Anaphora.** "Break of this level" (شکست این ناحیه) is resolved to the level in the previous sentence. These rows are marked `confidence=medium`.
- **Manual review.** I read every 2022 and 2023–2025 row against its post, and every 2026 row in compact form. I fixed about 120 posts by hand, listed in `MANUAL`: wrong side, descriptive levels taken as triggers, header/text symbol mismatches, and news or quiz posts.
- **Spot check.** On a random 30-row sample after the fixes, side and trigger were correct on 30/30. Role labels (risky vs main) are less reliable, about 1 in 15 doubtful.

Columns that go beyond the requested set:
- `record_type`: setup or chart_only.
- `trigger_role`: risky/early, main or first sign.
- `break_confirmation`: `break` for a touch or stop order, `close` for a candle close beyond the level (تثبیت).
- `conditional`, `validity_level`, `box`, `restated_from`, `outcome_post_ids`, `repost_ids`, `in_B_file`, `url`, `notes`.
- `clause_fa`: the Persian sentence the row came from.

## 3. Risk parameters: stops, R:R and targets

Calls almost never carry a stop. Only **8 of 728 rows** have one:
- **VIP templates (2022).** `#avaxusdt #short Entry market Sl: 77.13 1.04% Ts: 75.56 Tp1: 74.81` (8496) and `#ethusdt #short sl: 2808.60 1.5% / ts: 2730 / tp1: 2683` (8498). The stated stop is **1.04 % and 1.5 %** from entry. VIP marketing says «استاپ لاس های سیگنال ها نهایتن ۳،۴ ٪ هستش» ("signal stops are at most 3–4 %", 8319).
- **Spot or long-term buys.** These have wide structural stops: BLUR 2758 → stop 1800 (−35 %, 19070); SOL 179.71 → "safe" stop 104 and "risky" stop 143 (22992); RENDER 4.463 → 2.756 (23278); ARKM 0.1459 → 0.1162 risky or 0.0936 safe (26335). In the stated-stop rows, the median stop distance is **36 %** (n=6, range 5–42 %). That is spot investing, not the futures trigger method.
- **Futures triggers (2026).** I found one numeric stop: PI short 0.1127, existing shorts move stops above 0.1184 (+5.1 %, 26770). Otherwise stop size is discussed only qualitatively:
  - «اگه قیمت یکم بیشتر رنج بزنه و یه سقف پایینتر ایجاد بکنه، سایز استاپ خیلی بهتری خواهیم داشت» (25845): wait for a lower high, which gives a smaller stop.
  - «فعلا سایز استاپ خوبی نداریم … ترجیح میدم قیمت یکم زیر 2186 رنج بزنه و یه استاپ کوچک تر بهمون بده» (25765).
  - «چون پوزیشن براساس مومنتوم باز میشه، بهتره استاپ کوچیک بزاریم» (26051): momentum trades get small stops.
  - «قانون ۶ ارشیا عزیزپور: وقتی استاپ … زیادی بزرگه … باید صبر کنم» (25630): if the structural stop is too big, wait.
- **Relative to box size.** This cannot be measured from the text, because stops and boxes are never stated together. Two proxies:
  - Box width, where a box is given: median **4.0 %** of the trigger price (n=24, IQR 3.4–8.5 %).
  - Validity level (the opposite-side "valid while above/below X"), the closest thing to a logical stop: median **5.4 %** from the trigger (n=9, IQR 2.8–7.7 %).

**R:R.** Only 3 setup rows state one:
- RR ≈ 5 achieved (17827).
- "تریگر ورود ARUSDT | ریسک به ریوارد 3" (24491).
- RR 2 and 3 on RENDER/CFG (26154).

The house rules are «تارگت هم ریسک به ریوارد 2 تا 3 باشه» for fakeout candles (14733), «مینیموم ریسک به ریوارد 2» for counter-trend shorts (26043), and "first targets are always RR 2" in the 2022 VIP (8882). The **2022 VIP log** (8302) lists 186 signals: 67 at −2/−3 %, 77 wins at RR ≥ 2, 35 "ts" (scalp target or trailing), 2 risk-free, 2 tp-scalp and 1 cancelled. Among the 77 wins, RR 2 is by far the most common, with a tail to RR 9/10/12/14/18/24.

**Targets.** 54 rows (7 %) give one; 1 row gives two. Targets are the next S/R level («قیمت میتونه به سمت X حرکت کنه»), not R multiples. The first target is a median **8.2 %** from the trigger (n=49, IQR 5.1–11.6 %). Multi-target ladders appear only as Fibonacci-extension spot targets (RENDER 14.995 / 22.292 / 39.204 / 80, 23278) and in the 2022 VIP Tp1 + TS template.

## 8. Mechanical definitions inferred from the examples (labels are mine)

Parameters marked † are my operationalisation; the posts do not fix them.

1. **range_breakout_trigger** (the core method).
   - Draw the latest range or box, or the last swing high/low, on the signal timeframe (alts 1h/4h, majors 4h/1d).
   - Long trigger = the box top or last minor high; short trigger = the box bottom or last minor low.
   - Enter on a stop order through the level (2026 default) or on a candle close beyond it (تثبیت, the 2022 default).
   - Tiers: an *early/risky* trigger is the nearer minor level, a *main* trigger is the box edge or major S/R, and a *confirmation* is a HH/HL (or LH/LL) beyond the main level.
   - Filters:
     - Rising volume into or through the level.
     - At least a second touch of the level.
     - Direction of the higher-timeframe trend.
     - Not on weekends.
     - BTC and dominance context for alts.

2. **momentum_trigger_rsi.** The range breakout plus an RSI(14†) condition on the same timeframe:
   - long: RSI crosses above 70, or above a stated horizontal RSI level (e.g. 72.73, 75.58);

3. **ma_pullback** (rare, a confirmation). In a momentum leg, price pulls back to SMA7 or SMA25 (SMA99 for the bigger trend). Entry is a setup candle or the break of the pullback's minor high or low, in the trend direction.
4. **fakeout.** The level is broken, then a strong candle closes back inside the range. Trade the opposite way: enter on that candle or on the break of the range edge back inside. SL beyond the fake candle's wick; target RR 2–3 (14733).
5. **indecision_trigger** ("کندل ستاپ" or ایندیسیژن). An indecision or setup candle forms at the level. Once it closes, place a stop order beyond its wick in the trade direction, with SL at the other side of the candle (26143; education channel T1).
6. **pullback.** After a breakout, the retest of the broken level, with a confirmation candle or volume, is the entry. It is offered as the "missed trigger" alternative (25764, 26252).
7. **pattern.** The trigger is the activation level of a drawn pattern: trend line, channel edge, wedge, triangle side, H&S neckline or V-pattern high. It is used mostly in 2022. In 2026 a pattern break usually comes with an explicit price trigger.
