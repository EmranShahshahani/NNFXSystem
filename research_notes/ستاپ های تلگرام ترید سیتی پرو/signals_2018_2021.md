# Trade City Pro (@tradecity_pro): trade calls and setups, 2018–2021

**Source.** Input is `data/cache/telegram/main_signals_A_2018_2021.jsonl`: 456 posts, ids 1561–8087, dated 2019-06-22 → 2021-12-27. Follow-ups and context come from the full archive `main_all.jsonl`. Post URL = `https://t.me/tradecity_pro/<id>`.

**Outputs, all in this folder:**

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `extract_signals_2018_2021.py` | The pipeline. It normalises digits, de-duplicates, classifies, regex-extracts, and applies the manual-review table that is embedded in the script. It also searches follow-ups and writes the stats. |
| `signals_2018_2021.csv` | One row per **call leg**. A post with two scenarios (e.g. "long above X / short below Y") becomes two legs. |
| `signals_2018_2021_review.txt` | Per post: the text, the auto-parse, the final legs, and follow-up candidates from the archive. Use it to audit the manual decisions. |
| `signals_2018_2021_stats.json` | Every number in this note. |
| `signals_2018_2021_archive_supplement.csv` | Call-like posts that are in the archive but **not** in the input subset. Auto-parsed and **not** manually reviewed (see §0.3). |

**CSV columns.** The requested columns are `post_id, date_utc, symbol, timeframe, side, setup_type, entry, entry_type, stop, targets, stated_rr, conditions, outcome_reported, confidence`. I added these after them:

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `leg` | Leg number within the post. |
| `url` | Link to the post. |
| `risk_pct` | Account risk as stated in the post. |
| `stop_pct` | Stated stop %, or computed from the stated entry and stop. |
| `rr_tp1_calc` | Reward/risk to the first target, **computed** from stated numbers (zone mid-point used as entry). For the 2021 market-entry template, the entry is implied from the stated stop price and stop %. |
| `n_targets` | Number of targets. |
| `completeness` | Which of entry / stop / target the text gives. |
| `entry_mode` | `immediate` (market), `resting` (limit/zone/plain price), or `conditional` (stop order or trigger). |
| `call_origin` | See the list below. |
| `repost_ids` | Ids of reposts of the same post. |
| `has_photo` | Whether the post had a photo. |

`call_origin` takes four values:
- `signal`: a terse numeric signal.
- `analysis`: a prose setup.
- `position_note`: the author's own position management.
- `example`: a didactic post labelled "not for trading".

Statistics below use `signal` + `analysis` only: **213 legs from 175 posts**.

**Rules followed.**
- No number was invented. A value that is not in the text is left blank.
- Levels that exist only on a chart image are noted in `conditions` and not filled in.
- Persian (۰–۹) and Arabic-Indic (٠–٩) digits, the Arabic decimal sign `٫` and thousands commas (`3,138,144`) are normalised before parsing.
- Values written without a decimal point are kept as written and flagged: `2324` for BAT, `2304` for IOTA, `3182` for IOST.
- Two typos are corrected and documented in `conditions`: `Entry:026033` → 0.26033 (#4145), and `6921` → 0.06921 (#3812).

## 4. Completeness, conditionality and outcomes

### Completeness

- **Complete (entry + stop + ≥ 1 target): 70 / 213 legs (33%).**
- Entry + stop only: 33. Entry + target: 14. Stop + target: 4. One field only: 48. Direction only: 44.
- **Signals are complete; analyses mostly are not:**
  - `signal`: 42 / 56 complete (75%).
  - `analysis`: 28 / 157 complete (18%), and 43 analyses are direction-only.
- By year, the complete share falls from 2019 (15/36) and 2020 (34/69) to 2021 (21/108). This is driven by the Nov-2021 two-sided template and BTC "view" posts.
- **Conditional vs immediate.** 57% of legs (122) are conditional (stop or trigger entry: "if it closes above…"). 27% are resting orders at a stated price or zone and 16% are market entries. Among complete legs, the split is resting 38, immediate 17, conditional 15.
- **Account risk** is stated on 52 legs (24%). Leverage is mentioned as 3–10. From 2021, "risk 1%" and "≤ 3% of capital" recur: «مدیریت سرمایه فراموش نشه که در صورت خوردن حد ضرر فقط ۳٪ سرمایه ضرر کنید» (#4720: "remember money management: lose only 3% of capital if stopped").

### Outcomes reported in later posts

I searched the archive for up to 30 days after each call (same asset + outcome words) and read every candidate. **30 of 175 call posts (31 legs, 17%) have a reported outcome.** Specific posts are cited in `outcome_reported`; aggregate logs are cited only where the symbol is unique.

- **Positive reports: 27.** Examples:
  - #3214 → #3215 «تارگت 2 خورد و با لورج 10 نزدیک 23% سود داد» ("TP2 hit, ~23% with lev 10").
  - #3789 → #3790 "TP1 hit, ~15%".
  - #4053 → #4082 "TP1 33200 hit".
  - #4776 → #5556 "~27%, RR ~5".
  - #4827 → #4870 "both targets 36200 and 40000 hit".
  - #6449 → #6580 "57000 box reached".
- **Losses: 3.** #4014 (weekly log #4036 «zilusdt long STOP LOSS»); #3800 (probable, #3810 «حد ضرر خورد و 1% ضرر کردیم» with no symbol); #4220 (probable, monthly log #4317 «compusdt sl (-3% on balance)»).
- **Same-minute "hits".** Several hits were posted in the same minute as the call: #3690→#3691, #3707→#3708, #3709→#3710, #3711→#3712, #3727→#3728, #3821→#3822. The public post evidently re-published a VIP call that had already run, so these say nothing about tradability from the public post.
- **Reporting is one-sided.** The 27:3 ratio among reported outcomes conflicts with the channel's own logs, which show 18–33% stop-outs (below). Unreported calls should not be assumed winners.
- Profit % are on margin at leverage 10 unless stated («درصد ها با لورج 10 حساب شدند», #2035), not price moves.

## 5. Mechanical definitions of the recurring setups (**inferred**)

These rules are inferred from the examples cited. They are not stated by the channel as rules. Parameters marked † are my choice where the text is silent. See `education_channel_rules.md` for the 2021 education-channel rules: min RR 2, 3% risk cap, stop beyond the wick of the trigger candle.

**S1. Level breakout / close-confirmation (`range_breakout`; 67 legs: #2467, #2737, #2985, #3124, #3251, #3354/2, #4720, #4776, #4782, #5828, #7033 …) — inferred.**
- Inputs: a horizontal level L (prior high/low, box edge, channel edge, "zone"), on timeframe T. T is usually 4h or 1D; 15m/1h for BTC intraday.
- Long: (a) a candle on T *closes* above L («بالای L ببنده / تثبیت بده»), or (b) a buy-stop/stop-limit order at L or just above it («stop buy / buy stop / stop limit بعد از L»). Short is the mirror: close below L or a sell-stop.
- Stop: a fixed % (stated values: 2–3%, median 3% for analyses), or below the next structure, or a close-based rule ("daily close below X"). Typical L-to-stop distance is 2–3%†.
- Targets: next resistance or previous high. 1–3 targets; TP1 is often 1–2R.
- 2021 two-sided variant (T5): one box, long above the upper level, short below the lower level, no stop or target given. Treat it as an OCO pair of stop orders at the two levels, with the stop at the opposite level or box mid†.

**S2. Trend-line break (`trendline_break`; 20 legs: #2309, #2956, #3225, #3231, #3596, #4412 …) — inferred.**
- Inputs: a descending trend line (for longs) through ≥ 2 swing highs†, on T = 15m (BTC intraday) or 4h/1D (alts).
- Entry: the first candle on T that closes beyond the line, or a stop-buy just above the line / the last high (#3231: 8810).
- Stop: fixed 1.6–3% (#2956, #3225), or below the last swing low.
- Targets: often not given ("will update"), or fib extensions (#3596).

**S3. Break → pullback → confirmation (`pullback`; 14 legs: #2498, #3172, #3209, #6182, #6353 …) — inferred.**
1. A level, trend line or MA (SMA99) is broken on T.
2. Price returns to it within a few bars.
3. Enter when a "good"/supportive candle («كندل خوب / كندل حمايتي») closes at the retest on T or a lower TF, or with a resting order at the retest price (#3209 at market 8760; #3188 limit at 669).
- Stop: below the retest low or previous low (#3209: 8400, ~4%).
- Targets: previous high and fib levels.
- Variant: #3209 adds a second entry on the break of the retest high (8840, stop 8600).

**S4. Indecision-candle trigger (`indecision_trigger`; 4 legs plus filters: #2527, #2540, #2586, #3135) — inferred.**
- Context: price at support/resistance, a trend line, or SMA99 (4h/1h/1D).
- Trigger: an indecision candle, i.e. small body relative to its range with wicks on both sides (body ≤ 1/3 of range†), forms at the level.
- Entry, two versions:
  - Aggressive: a zone order at the level while the candle forms (#2527: zone 2953–2957; #2540: sell zone 7300–7310).
  - Conservative: a stop entry beyond the indecision candle's opposite extreme. #2586: «زمانی که کندل روزانه فردا زیر 7375 اومد» ("when tomorrow's daily candle comes below 7375").
- Optional filter: RSI touching or reacting at a Bollinger band computed on RSI (#2527).
- Stop: beyond the zone. Stated stops are 1.2–2.6%. The education channel says to put it below the candle's wick.
- Targets: 3 targets at roughly 0.3R, 0.6R and 1–1.5R.

**S5. Candle-close trigger (`candle_trigger`; 8 legs, 2021: #3914, #3995, #4014, #5600, #5607, #5702, #6994) — inferred.**
- Context: price at a level (support/resistance or a broken level).
- Long when the trigger candle on T closes bullish, then enter at market. Trigger candles in the examples: 15m green close; 4h close above X; next candle engulfing the current one; a "very good" daily close.
- Stop: below that candle's low/wick («استاپ زیر کندل / زیر شدو»), or a stated level.
- Target: next level, or exit when a lower high forms (#3995).

**S6. Moving-average signals (`ma_signal`; 5 legs, 2019–2020) — inferred.**
- (a) Long when SMA7 crosses above SMA25 on 4h/1D (#2580, #2643). The safe entry is on a break of a nearby level.
- (b) Short when a 1h candle closes below SMA99, or on a pullback to SMA99 from below (#3472, #2586 leg 2, #2752). Optional confirmation: 4h SMA7/SMA25 bearish cross, or a 4h close below SMA25.
- Stop: above the recent swing (#3472: 216), or 3%.

**S7. RSI–Bollinger momentum (`momentum_rsi`; 1 primary leg, #2487; filter in #2527, #2601, #2985) — inferred.**
- An RSI(14)† with Bollinger bands applied to the RSI.
- Short: RSI fails to cross back above its BB mid-line, plus low-volume up-candles and price under SMA7/25. Entry is a sell zone just above price.
- Long confirmation: RSI crosses above its BB mid-line (#2985).

**S8. Patterns (`pattern`; 9 legs) — inferred.**
- Entry on the neckline or pattern-boundary break with volume (#3320, entry 5660).
- Or on the pullback to the broken neckline (#3289, entry 202.5–202.6).
- Stop at the previous swing low (196.7) or below the pattern (#3320: 4985, 12%).
- Targets: measured move or next levels.

**S9. 2021 market-scalp template (`market_scalp`; 9 legs, all long) — inferred.**
- Entry at market.
- Stop stated as both % and price: 0.7–2.8% (median 1.7%).
- Exits:
  - "tp scalp" ≈ 0.25–0.9R and "tp1" ≈ 1.8–2.5R (≈ 2R), computed from the implied entry.
  - "tp2: hold" is a trailed runner.
  - Partial closes of 30% at TP1 and 30% more later (#7404).
- Risk 0.5–1%.

**S10. 2019–2020 bare ladder (`unstated`; 51 legs).**
- No rationale.
- Entry price or zone, stop 1–7%.
- 4–16 targets spaced about 0.1–0.3R apart.
- Team states it does not trade them (#2869). Do not treat as a strategy.

## 6. Caveats

- `setup_type` is my label. It is read from the post's own wording ("شکست", "پولبک", "ایندسیژن", "تثبیت بالای", "sma99"…). When the text gives no reason, the label is `unstated`.
- `support_resistance` is the generic "buy at support / sell at resistance" bucket. It often has a candle confirmation that the post does not define.
- Side is blank for 6 legs where the text does not say long or short (#3230, #3882–3885, #3991). For #3320 and #4053 side is inferred from wording and targets, and this is noted in `conditions`.
- Outcome labels marked "probable" rest on follow-ups that don't name the symbol, or on aggregate logs matched by symbol. The other outcome labels cite a specific post.
- The archive supplement CSV is unreviewed regex output. Check it before using it in statistics.
